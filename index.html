Privacy Policy

Hymn Index · Effective 3 November 2026

This policy explains how the Hymn Index app handles information. The app is made and run by Reuben Pooley, referred to below as the Developer, or as “we”. Using the app means you accept what is described here.

Summary

The app does not collect, store or share any personal information. There are no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no third-party tracking. The hymn index is bundled inside the app. Tune audio is streamed when you ask for it. There is an optional Give a little page that opens PayPal's own page in your browser. The app never sees what you enter there.

Information we collect

None. The app does not ask for, gather or transmit anything personal or identifying. It does not reach your contacts, location, files, microphone, camera or device identifiers.

The app keeps a few things on your own device so it works the way you left it: your favourite tunes, the tunes you played and the searches you made recently, where you left off in a tune, whether you've seen the welcome cards, whether you asked for a giving reminder, and 2 numbers that pace the giving page. It also keeps a cache of tune audio, up to about 500 MB, so a tune you've played doesn't download twice. All of this stays in the app's own storage and is never sent to us. Android can clear the cache when your phone runs short of space.

What the app contains is an index: hymn numbers, metres, categories and tune names. It does not contain hymn words, authors or sheet music.

Permissions

The app has 9 Android permissions. Two are for streaming: internet access (INTERNET) and a check on network connectivity (ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE). Three let a tune keep playing when the app is in the background: FOREGROUND_SERVICE, FOREGROUND_SERVICE_MEDIA_PLAYBACK and WAKE_LOCK. POST_NOTIFICATIONS is asked for only when you tap “Remind me in 3 months”, so the app can show that reminder. RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED keeps a reminder you asked for after the phone restarts. BIND_GET_INSTALL_REFERRER_SERVICE lets the app ask the Play Store app on your phone, once after install, whether you came from a shared tune link, so it can open that tune. The answer stays on your phone. The last, DYNAMIC_RECEIVER_NOT_EXPORTED_PERMISSION, is added by the Android libraries the app is built with. It keeps an internal message inside the app and grants access to nothing on your device. None of the 9 gives the app your contacts, location, files, microphone, camera or device identifiers.

Reminders, updates and cars

If you ask for a giving reminder, the app shows a notification every 3 months until you stop it. You can stop it on the Give a little page or in Android's settings.

The app asks the Play Store app on your phone whether a newer version is available, and if one is, offers to open the Play Store. Nothing about this is sent to us.

When your phone is connected to a car, a watch or headphones, they can show the tune that's playing and play, pause or skip it, as your lock screen does. Nothing about this is sent to us.

Internet access and audio streaming

Internet access is used only to stream hymn tune audio on demand from a content delivery host, Cloudflare R2 object storage. When you tap play, the app asks that host for the relevant audio file.

As with any request to a server, the hosting provider may record standard technical request data in its logs, such as your IP address, the time of the request and the file requested, for delivery and security. That data is handled by the hosting provider under its own terms. The app does not use it to identify you, build a profile, or track you across services.

Optional support

The app has a Give a little page. If you tap Give with PayPal, PayPal's own page opens in your browser over the app. Any payment details you enter go to PayPal, under PayPal's own terms and privacy statement. The app never sees them and doesn't know whether you gave. A monthly gift is a PayPal plan you can cancel in PayPal at any time. Giving unlocks no features; every feature works the same for everyone.

If you do contribute, PayPal gives the Developer the details it gives any payment recipient, such as your name, your email address and the amount. Those details stay with the Developer. We may use your email address to thank you for your contribution. We do not add you to a mailing list, we do not share, sell or pass the details to anyone, and we never link them to how you use the app.

How information is used

The app does not use personal information for any purpose, because it collects none. Audio requests are used only to deliver the tune you asked for.

Data sharing and sale

We do not sell, rent or share personal data. The app collects none, and the only personal details we ever receive are the ones PayPal gives us if you choose to contribute.

Children’s privacy

The app's target audience on Google Play is users aged 13 and over. It does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Data retention and deletion

The app holds no personal data, so there is nothing for us to retain or delete. Uninstalling the app removes it, its cached audio, your favourites and its settings from your device.

If you have contributed through PayPal and want the payer details PayPal gave us removed from our records, email the address below and we will delete them.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Any change is posted on this page with a new effective date.
