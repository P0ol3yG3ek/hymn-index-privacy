Privacy Policy

Hymn Index · Effective 2 June 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the Hymn Index mobile application (the “App”) handles information. The App is provided by Reuben Pooley (the “Developer”). By using the App you agree to the practices described below.

Summary

The App does not collect, store, or share any personal information. There are no user accounts, no analytics, no advertising, and no third-party tracking. All hymn content is bundled inside the App and read from your device.

Information we collect

None. The App does not ask for, gather, or transmit any personal or identifying information. It does not access your contacts, location, files, microphone, camera, or device identifiers.

Internet access and audio streaming

The App requests a single permission, INTERNET. This is used for one purpose only: to stream hymn tune audio on demand from a content delivery host (Cloudflare R2 object storage). When you tap play, the App requests the relevant audio file from that host.

As with any request to a server, the hosting provider may automatically record standard technical request data — such as your IP address, the time of the request, and the file requested — in its server logs for delivery and security purposes. This data is handled by the hosting provider under its own privacy terms and is not used by the App to identify you, build a profile, or track you across services.

How information is used

The App does not use personal information for any purpose, because it does not collect any. Audio requests are used solely to deliver the requested tune to your device.

Data sharing and sale

We do not sell, rent, or share any personal data, because we do not collect any.

Children’s privacy

The App is suitable for users of all ages and does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children.

Data retention and deletion

Because the App stores no personal data, there is nothing for us to retain or delete. Uninstalling the App removes its bundled content from your device.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with a revised effective date.

Contact

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, contact the Developer at reubengpooley@gmail.com.
